## Decision

Authorize the exact five archived bodies and bounded DCN index metadata query
in the [fixture proposal](../investigations/2026/v2-new-source-fixture-proposal-2026-09-13.md)
and its machine allowlist. The shared limits are 12 HTTP requests, 32 MiB and
15 minutes. The metadata query permits at most its probe and page zero.

This authorizes no DCN PDFs, additional index bodies, origin requests or
production acquisition. Bodies remain in fixture quarantine. It accepts no
year, activates no page-kind policy, creates no permanent phase-two watch and
authorizes no publication. The runner must retain existing host budgets,
spacing, controls and source binding.

## Why

New source contracts need complete real controls before enforcement. The normal
history gate requires year acceptance and page-kind enforcement before fetching.
The owner approved this narrow exception after reviewing its concrete scope.
Additional fixtures need their own reviewable proposals and explicit decisions.
