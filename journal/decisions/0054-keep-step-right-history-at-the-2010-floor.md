# D-0054: Keep Step Right history at the 2010 floor

Recorded: 2026-09-17  
Decided by: owner, 2026-09-17, Sandile Keswa; source: Owner reply in the implementation session: “Keep 2010–2016; correct WP14 criterion”  
Topic: Historical scope  
Supersedes: —  
Superseded by: —

## Decision

WP14 covers Step Right events from 2010 through 2016. Correct its completion
criterion to match the accepted 2010-01-01 history floor; do not acquire 2009
events under this work package.

## Why

The accepted overall history plan starts in 2010, but WP14's criterion said
2009–2016. The owner resolved the discrepancy in favor of the existing floor.
This is a scope correction, not acceptance of any year's event inventory.
