# Investigations and dated outcomes

These records explain what was examined and what the evidence showed. They
preserve their original dates and limits. A recommendation is not an accepted
decision, and a successful test is not proof of deployment.

## Browse by question

- [Collection and historical sources](collection.md): Find source surveys, archive coverage, parser preparation, and fixture investigations.
- [Person matches and corrections](identity.md): Find identity reviews, withdrawn matches, correction releases, and evaluation packets.
- [Worker state, controls, and scheduling](runtime.md): Find investigations of unfinished work, retries, pause controls, saved outputs, and scheduling.
- [Builds, releases, and operating handoffs](releases.md): Find build measurements, release checks, and dated operating handoffs. Current status lives in the docs.
- [Dataset quality and missing results](quality.md): Find data-quality audits and investigations of missing evidence.
- [Hosting and database migration](hosting.md): Find hosting options, PostgreSQL experiments, and storage research.
- [Competition-rule audits](rules.md): Find evidence for the written WSDC rules and unresolved historical questions.
- [Documentation](documentation.md): Find investigations and outcomes about how the project is explained.
