# Why result-site events do not attach to their WSDC event

Date: 2026-10-13 (UTC), production read around 20:00 UTC  
Type: Investigation  
Topic: Event identity  
Related plan or decision: maintenance item "Attach result-site events to their WSDC event" in [plans](../../../docs/plans/README.md); D-0180 (series alias review), D-0191, D-0222; [release losses](release-losses-2026-10-12.md)

## Summary

1. Two parts of the pipeline decide which event a results-site listing belongs to, and they use different rules. The **inventory** (`project/history.py`) matches a listing to a WSDC series through `series_aliases.csv` and registry names, then copies the listing's source and dates onto the WSDC event. The **map** (`project/map.py`) decides where the listing's contests go, and it only accepts an exact normalized name plus overlapping dates. "Liberty Swing" is not "Liberty Swing Dance Championships", so the map makes a second event, `2026-06-liberty-swing`. That is why the WSDC row says `source = eepro` while the contests sit elsewhere.
2. A second defect is bigger. When a results placeholder exists before the registry occurrence, the inventory adopts the placeholder as the occurrence but never changes its `wsdc_status` from `unknown`. In release `3fe7bae`, 67 of the 2026 events with results are real registry occurrences with registry points on the same row, labelled `unknown`. The Chicago Classic, Florida Dance Magic, Swingover, Derby City Swing, Paris Swing Classic and Phoenix 4th of July are all here. They do not need a merge, only the right label.
3. A third defect inflates the count. The inventory reads every platform listing as a `registry` listing, so NJ Hustle Congress and Disco America, both non-WSDC events, count as WSDC events with results. They are 2 of the "12 of 62".
4. All 89 of the 2026 result events with `unknown` status in the release have a WSDC counterpart. 67 are the occurrence itself, 12 split pairs are high confidence, 1 is medium (River Swing Nights), and 9 have a counterpart in live state that the release withholds. The problem is not 2026-only: 2025 and 2024 show the same split and label defects. In the release, no 2024 or 2025 event has both contests and registry points.
5. Recommended: one project code change that lets the map use the inventory's series-and-month rule, sets `registry` when the inventory adopts a placeholder, and stops labelling platform listings `registry`. For 2026 in the release, past WSDC events with results rise from 12 of 62 to about 89 of 126, and events with both results and registry points rise from 8 to 87.

## How an event ID is chosen

| What                                    | Who decides                                        | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Contests, rounds, entries, placements   | `project/event` unit through `source_event_map`    | `contests.py:873-880` reads the observations of every listing mapped to the event. The map row comes from `map.propose` (`map.py:87-142`): an `event_aliases.csv` or `source_urls.csv` override, else the one catalog event (`wsdc_status != 'unknown'`, `:92`) whose `normalize_event_name` equals the listing's and whose dates overlap (`:113-120`), else a generated id `<end month>-<series slug>` with a placeholder event (`:121-141`, `ids.py:37-49`). |
| The listing's header facts on the event | Inventory (`prepare_inventory`)                    | Each `source_events` row is read as a listing (`history.py:150-173`). Its series is the `series_aliases.csv` row for its series slug and month (`:188-189`, `_alias_for` `:535-543`), else the one registry series with that printed-name slug (`:67`). Its event is the one held event of that series in the end month or the next (`:215-236`). The upsert merges `sources`, sets `source`, `snapshot_id` and the dates (`:290-330`).                        |
| Registry placements                     | Year units (`finalize_year`, `history.py:384-405`) | Series id plus reporting month, first event id in sort order.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Calendar rows                           | Calendar unit (`events.py:92-101`)                 | Joins a held occurrence only by equal series slug in the end month or the next; ignores aliases.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

**Why the map misses.** `normalize_event_name` (`normalize/events.py:21-26`) only lowercases and strips punctuation. It keeps every word, including the year ("Dutch Open 2026"). Results sites print short names (GNDC, MADjam, Swingtacular), so the exact test fails for almost every pair. `propose` never reads `series_aliases.csv`, though the file is already a recipe input of every project unit. Date windows and month-only dates are not the cause: in all 26 live 2026 pairs, the dates of the listing and the WSDC row are the same, because the inventory copied them.

**Why some results land on the WSDC row anyway.** When the generated id equals the inventory's id (same month, same series slug, as with "Derby City Swing"), `resolve` takes the inventory's row (`map.py:182-188`). Results and points then share one row. But that row began life as the map's placeholder with status `unknown`. The registry occurrence adopted it, either through the legacy branch (`history.py:78-95`, `UPDATE ... SET series_id, event_month, held='held'`) or through the insert's `ON CONFLICT` (`:108-111`). Neither sets `wsdc_status`. The listing upsert then keeps the status of every held row (`:315`, `CASE WHEN events.held='held' THEN events.wsdc_status`). So the `unknown` label is frozen. Example: `2026-01-derby-city-swing`, series `wsdc-242`, held, 24 contests, 82 registry placements, `unknown`.

**Why the WSDC row says `source = eepro`.** The inventory resolves `eepro:libertyswing2026` ("Liberty Swing") through the reviewed alias row `Liberty Swing,wsdc-96,eepro` to the held occurrence `2026-06-liberty-swing-dance-championships`, and writes `eepro` into its `sources`, `source` and dates. The map never sees that decision. It proposes the generated id `2026-06-liberty-swing` (`name_date`, confidence 0.5, with a placeholder), and the 29 contests follow the map.

**Why two hustle events count as WSDC.** The inventory builds each platform listing as `CalendarRow(..., "registry", ...)` (`history.py:164-170`). A listing whose series is a `listed-*` alias gets `wsdc_status = registry` on its own row. In live state, 11 such 2026 events are labelled `registry` (NJ Hustle Congress, Disco America, eight bachata events, one SILC event).

## Measurement

**Method.** For each event with contests and `wsdc_status = unknown`, I looked for a WSDC event (status `registry` or `trial`, or a held row with a `wsdc-*` series) in the same year or a neighbouring year with dates within 3 days. A month-only occurrence had to be in the listing's end month or the next month. The names had to share a word other than filler words ("swing", "dance", "open", "wcs", ...), or one name had to contain the other, or a `series_aliases.csv` row had to name the series. **High** means all of that, plus the inventory's own route (alias or registry name) resolves the listing to that series, plus the WSDC row already carries the listing's source and dates. **Medium** means dates and name only. I checked the routes with the deployed `series_slug` and `_alias_for` against live registry names.

Merging at build time, or teaching `normalize_event_name` to drop words, are worse. The first hides a project defect. The second guesses: "German Open" is also a bachata and a TAF event on scoring.dance.

**What (b) changes.**

1. Move the listing-to-event rule out of `prepare_inventory` (`history.py:185-236`) into one function both units call. Given a listing name and end month, it returns the one event of the listing's series in that month or the next. The series comes from an alias row or from a unique registry name. It returns nothing when the result is ambiguous.
2. In `map.propose` (`map.py:109-142`), when the exact name test finds nothing, call that function, and propose the event it returns with a new match method, such as `series_month`. Count held rows in the catalog whatever their status (`:92`). Read registry names from `series` and held events, which the inventory writes and the mapping already depends on, not from `registry_placements`. Otherwise every dancer update would re-run the mappings.
3. In the inventory, set `wsdc_status = 'registry'` when adoption finds `unknown` (`history.py:93` and `:111`), and let a held row whose status is `unknown` take the listing's status (`:315`).
4. Give platform listings the status of their series (`registry` for `wsdc-*`, else `unknown`) instead of a fixed `"registry"` (`history.py:164-170`).

**What moves.** In live state, 81 placeholder events disappear: their `resolution` units find no proposal and release the row (`map.py:302-349`). In 2026 alone, 389 contests and 16,610 entries move, along with their rounds, placements and judges. The 2025 pairs carry 614 contests and 25,789 entries, and the 2024 pairs 142 and 8,229. Contest, round and entry ids change their event prefix, the changelog records the renames, and link units re-link the moved entries. Registry placements do not move, since they are already on the WSDC rows. At least 185 held rows get `registry` (those with results).

**Risks.**

- _False merges._ The rule adds no new identity guess. It reuses the series decisions the inventory already publishes in `events.sources`, and the alias rows are D-0180 reviews. A mistaken alias would now move contests as well as header facts, so the preflight should list every proposed move.
- _Withholding._ Results moved onto a row whose header the builder rejects disappear from the release. 9 such 2026 WSDC rows are withheld today (parser 3 headers), while their placeholders publish 81 contests. Re-admit the parser 3 `scoringdance.event` units first (fix 2 in [release losses](release-losses-2026-10-12.md)), or replay the partitions before publishing.
- _The release gate._ The release loses up to 35 placeholder rows (13 for 2026, 20 for 2025, 2 for 2024). `decrease:events` will refuse unless other growth covers it, so an approval row may be needed.
- _Records and docs._ This changes an identity rule, so it needs a decision record. The same change must update `docs/reference/sources/eepro.md` section 4, `scoring-dance.md` section 4, the mapping section of `state.md` and the placeholder paragraph of `data-model.md`.

**How to verify.**

- _Offline test_ (`tests/test_project_map.py`). A calendar row "Liberty Swing Dance Championships", a registry occurrence `wsdc-96`, an eepro listing "Liberty Swing" and the alias row must leave one event that holds both the contests and the points, with status `registry`. A placeholder that the registry adopts must come out `registry`. A `listed-*` platform listing must stay `unknown`.
- _Production preflight receipt._ List the proposed map moves and confirm each is in this record's pair list.
- _Post-check receipt._ No `slug-*` event shares a source and dates with a held WSDC row. No held `wsdc-*` row with contests is `unknown`. Every moved contest count matches.

## Conclusion and follow-up

Implement (b) as release work, in one change with its decision record. Run the parser 3 re-admission first, or replay partitions before publishing. Add a reviewed `series_aliases.csv` row for River Swing Nights (`wsdc-395`). Later, let a listing join a not-yet-held row of its series in the same month (WCS Party, Global Grand Prix), and let the calendar unit use the same function (`events.py:92-101`).

## Rehearsal of the fix, all years (2026-10-13, 20:36 to 23:45 UTC)
