Date: 2026-09-18  
Type: Investigation appendix  
Related: [Conclusions and reduction conditions](pytest-suite-review-2026-09-18.md)

This is the initial assessment, before the owner chose a smaller default run.
The current implemented selection is [tests/suite.toml](../../../tests/suite.toml);
see the [testing guide](../../../docs/guides/testing.md) for core and extended commands.

Every collected test file appears once below. **Keep** means no justified reduction was found, not that every case is proven indispensable. **Consolidate** requires preserving the named behavior in a replacement. **Separate** keeps the tests with explicitly selected operational tooling. **Retire candidate** requires the conditions in the main report; obsolete executables and their tests must retire together.

The proposed routine counts are conditional planning estimates. For retired drivers, nonzero counts budget replacement tests in a shared owner; they do not identify exact surviving nodes in that old file. They sum to 2,529, with another 190 retained separately, for 2,719 maintained cases. The current suite remains 2,998.
