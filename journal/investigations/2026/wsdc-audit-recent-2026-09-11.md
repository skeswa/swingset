# Auditing written WSDC rules, 2024–2026

> Historical record. Dates, status claims, and instructions below describe the
> original work. See [current status](../../../docs/status.md) before acting.
> [Project journal](../../README.md)

Audited 2026-09-11 against the locally retained primary WSDC PDFs and newsletters. This is a claim-by-claim audit of the 2024–2026 portions, the current-rule claims elsewhere in the document, and the dataset conclusions that depend on this period. The design document was not edited.

## Result

The recent-history portion is substantially accurate, but it is not 100% correct. The audit found two material overstatements, two effective-date/version-description defects, one incomplete account of the 2024.2B changes, and several citation or wording defects. The most consequential dataset issue is the categorical statement that Trial Events have registry rows only from 2025-01-02: the newsletter announces January 2, the final rulebook is effective January 1, and its limiting footnote speaks about Trial Events approved after October 16, 2024. The evidence does not establish a universal first registry-row date.

## Recommended corrections to the design document

1. Qualify the 2024.1B row with its actual effective date, 2024-03-27.
2. Replace “2024.2B added footnotes only” with the actual July 11 clarifications: driving distance and the Raw Score Judge’s unique numerical raw-score duty.
3. Describe 2025 v4 as a draft, never an operative rules version.
4. Cite the WSDC rules index for the 2026-01-01 effective date and record that the 2026 PDFs’ terminal footer incorrectly says January 1, 2025.
5. Say 2026 B/C/D did not change points rules; do not say D changed only formatting and one definition.
6. Say the 2026 primary advancement chart and tier-points chart stayed unchanged from 2025. The new secondary-role advancement chart is a material change.
7. Add the “Trial Events approved after October 16, 2024” qualifier.
8. Preserve the January 1/January 2 Trial effective-date conflict and remove the unsupported assertion about the first registry-row date.
9. Amend the supposed all-era constant restricting points to Registry Events because eligible Trial Events award points from 2025.
10. Flag New Year events for explicit rule attribution. The outgoing-December posting rule does not establish which annual points chart governs them.

## Residual uncertainty

No reviewed primary document resolves the one-day Trial-points discrepancy or proves the first Trial Event row actually entered in the registry. The WSDC rules index establishes that the 2026 rules took effect on 2026-01-01, but the reviewed 2026 PDFs contain no clean revision history for versions 1A–1D and retain an internally wrong 2025 effective-date footer. The Trial-row date and version-specific publication chronology remain uncertain pending dated WSDC evidence or registry observation.
