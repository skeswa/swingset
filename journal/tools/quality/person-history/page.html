What is confirmed?

Registry results belong to WSDC #7849. The all-participation view groups corresponding registry and score-sheet records by event, month, division, role, and compatible result. This is a display association; it does not confirm the sheet identity. Score-sheet entries and judging appearances match “JesAnn Nail,” “Jes Ann Nail,” or “Jess Ann Nail,” but their identity links are unconfirmed in this release. What might be missing?

The dataset begins in 2010, and historical score-sheet coverage is incomplete. An absent result does not mean nonparticipation. “No registry match” means no corresponding row was found in this extract; it does not assert zero points. Prelims and semifinals are included whenever a sheet records them. Registry months are reported months, not exact dates. Registry and score-sheet records overlap and should not be added together as unique competitions. A best recorded round does not establish elimination.
