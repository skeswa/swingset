# WSDC rule changes, 2018-2023

This chronology records written competition rules and their evidence gaps.
Start with the [rule-history summary](../wsdc-rules-history.md) for the eras
and their effect on the dataset. Source labels refer to its [source ledger](../wsdc-rules-history.md#sources).

### 2018-01-03: the six-tier chart and the advancement chart

Points Registry Rules 2018.1A [P2018], announced at the 2017-11-25
membership meeting; database switched 2018-01-03 [NL2017-12 p.1 "New Six
Tier Point System"]. Built from an analysis of 2015 and 2016 results
[NL2017-12 p.1; NL2017-07 p.1 "Tier Point System"].

| Tier | Competitors per role | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | Extra                 |
| ---- | -------------------- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --------------------- |
| 1    | 5 to 10              | 3   | 2   | 1   | 0   | 0   | none                  |
| 2    | 11 to 19             | 6   | 4   | 3   | 2   | 1   | none                  |
| 3    | 20 to 39             | 10  | 8   | 6   | 4   | 2   | 1 point through 12th  |
| 4    | 40 to 79             | 15  | 12  | 10  | 8   | 6   | 1 point through 15th  |
| 5    | 80 to 129            | 20  | 16  | 14  | 12  | 10  | 2 points through 15th |
| 6    | 130+                 | 25  | 22  | 18  | 15  | 12  | 2 points through 15th |

Chart: [P2018 p.1 "Points Award per Tier Chart"].

- Minimum for points becomes five leaders and five followers in the
  division [P2018 p.1 "Tier Minimums and Guidelines"] (before: five
  couples [P2015b p.1]).
- A Jack and Jill Advancement Chart separates "allowed" from "required":
  Novice allowed at 16 Novice points, required at 30; Intermediate 30 /
  45; Advanced allowed at 45 and required at 60 Advanced points, both
  counted within 36 months [P2018 p.3 chart].
- All Star is no longer permanent. A dancer must earn 3 All Star points
  in a 36-month period to stay; otherwise they return to Advanced, and
  need 10 Advanced points within 12 months to come back [P2018 p.3 chart;
  NL2017-12 p.1]. The January 2019 newsletter clarified that the 36
  months run from the date All Star status was reached [NL2019-01 p.1
  "All Star Clarification"].
- Transition: a dancer who already had 15 Novice points by 2017-12-31
  could still dance Intermediate (with a petition showing the points).
  Everyone else needed 16 [NL2018-07 p.1 "Novice Competitor
  Clarification"].
- Champion points are tracked, but Champion is "a general category set by
  promoters" [P2018 p.3 chart note]. Age-based placements are stated as
  not recorded in this version [P2018 p.1 "Points Registry" note]
  (**unverified** whether Junior and Masters points were actually absent
  from the registry during 2018; they were "recorded" before [P2015b
  p.1] and are recorded again from 2019 [P2019 p.1, p.2 rule 9]).
- Formal petitions are limited to one level up or down and must be
  filed with the event report. A dancer entering a higher division
  without a formal petition earns no points if they reach the final
  [P2018 p.2 rule 3, Petitions 1-5].

The companion event rules retain the 120-day default penalty denying
recording of results and the preservation of points for events below the
60-competitor minimum [E2018 p.4 default note, p.5 Event Structure #4].
They no longer repeat the 2015 whole-event suppression clause for the
one-contest/both-role violations. That textual omission does not prove a
policy reversal [E2018 p.5 Competition Rules #1-3; R2015 p.4 §5 #2-3].

### 2019: Juniors and Masters, rounds, role switching

Points Registry Rules 2019.1A are dated 2019-02-15, but do not label
that date as a blanket effective date. Some items took effect 2019-07-01
[P2019 p.1 "Tier Size and Number of Competition Rounds"] or 2020-01-01
[NL2019-04 p.1 "New Rules as of 2019"].

- The registry tracks Juniors (17 or younger at event close) and Masters
  (50 or older) and records points for one Junior and one Masters contest
  per event [P2019 p.1 "Points Registry", p.2 rule 9].
- Tiers are explicitly per role, with the worked example still used
  today (18 leaders and 35 followers give Tier 2 and Tier 3) [P2019 p.1
  "Tiers and Points Awarded Per Tier"; R2026b §3.3.1].
- Advancement chart adds: a dancer with 1 or 2 All Star points may
  compete in Advanced or All Star [P2019 p.3 chart].
- Role switching within a contest is not allowed [NL2019-01 p.2 "Role
  Switching"]. A dancer may enter as either role where the event allows
  it, or traditional roles where it does not [P2019 p.2 Competitor 4].
- Number of rounds per tier becomes mandatory from 2019-07-01 [P2019 p.1;
  NL2019-04 p.1].
- Results are due within 5 days (was 10), with late fees, effective
  2019-01-01 [NL2019-01 p.1; P2019 p.2 Event Director 3].
- The registry website starts showing each dancer's computed level and
  eligibility flags [NL2019-01 p.1 "Check Your Skill Level"].
- The companion event rules prescribe loss of event status for surcharge
  delays over 120 days and result delays over 60 days. They do not repeat
  the earlier express denial of recording current results. Low-attendance
  and probation-year points remain protected [E2019 §§3-5]. Neither loss
  of status nor omission of an old clause proves which awards were posted.

### 2020-01-01: path to Champions and standardized callbacks

The cached file named 2020.1B internally identifies its points rules as
2020.1A [P2020]. No distinct substantive 1B points text was recovered.

- Events offering a WSDC Champion Jack & Jill must let All Stars with
  150 or more All Star points (no time limit) enter it. Not required to enter. Events
  may keep extra Champion qualifications of their own [P2020 p.2 rule 10,
  p.4 chart; NL2020-01 p.1-2 "New All Star Path to WSDC Champions", FAQ
  4].
- Prelim callback scoring standardized: Yes 10, Alternate 1 4.5,
  Alternate 2 4.3, Alternate 3 4.2, No 0 [NL2020-01 p.1-2 "New Callback
  System Standardization Requirement"; NL2022-09 p.2]. This decides who
  reaches the final and therefore who can earn points.
- The no-points penalty now explicitly covers dancing either up or down
  without a formal petition when the dancer reaches the final [P2020
  p.2 rule 3; P2018 p.2 Petitions for the earlier up-only wording].
- A dancer may compete in the opposite role in an age-based contest if
  the event permits [P2020 p.3 Competitor 4a].
- 2020-03: the 36-month windows for Advanced and All Star are frozen for
  the pandemic. Points keep accruing and none expire [R2023a Chart 3 note
  ‡ "effective March 2020 through December 2023"]. The freeze was
  extended through 2021 [A2021 p.2; NL2021-05 p.3], through 2023
  [NL2022-09 p.1 "WSDC extends freeze"]. On 2023-07-05 the windows
  were abolished, with a grace period for prior All Star qualification;
  the old expiry calculation did not resume [R2023b Chart 3 note ‡;
  NL2023-09 p.2].

### 2021: both roles at one event, points tracked per role

Announced February 2021; May 14, 2021 addendum to the 2020 rules [A2021
p.1; NL2021-05 p.3 "Non-dominant Role Competing and Tracking"]. The
exact effective day and the database deployment date are **unverified**;
February is the announcement month, not a proven day-level cutoff.

- A dancer may compete as both leader and follower in skill-level Jack &
  Jills at the same event. Optional for events [A2021 p.1].
- Leader and follower points are tracked separately. A dancer's level is
  the higher of the two roles [A2021 p.1 bullets 1-2]. Combined counting
  ends [A2021 p.1 example "Taylor"].
- The non-dominant role may dance one level down, but not in the same
  division as the dominant role. No petitions beyond one level down
  [A2021 p.1 bullets 3-4].
- One-time option for dancers whose combined points had already moved
  them up: stay up in the dominant role, or drop back for both roles
  [A2021 p.1 "grandfathered"].
- 2021 only: events need 60 unique competitors instead of 80 [A2021 p.2;
  NL2021-05 p.3]. The addendum also says no new Trial Events will be
  approved in 2021 [A2021 p.2].

### 2023-01-01: one rulebook, primary and secondary roles, Sophisticated

Registry Event Rules 2023.1B, dated 2022-11-02 [R2023a p.1, p.12]. First
document to combine event and points rules [NL2022-09 p.1; NL2023-01
p.1].

- Sophisticated (35 or older at event close) is tracked; one contest per
  event [NL2022-09 p.1; R2023a p.2 "Points Registry", §3.1]. The
  newsletter highlights that only a Masters contest open to all
  earns points [NL2022-09 p.1]. The rulebook applies the condition to
  all WSDC age-based contests: open to everyone meeting the age
  requirement [R2023a §3.1 #2b].
- Primary and secondary roles are formal terms. A dancer may enter at
  most two skill-level Jack & Jills, one per role, and must dance one
  level down in the secondary role. A dancer with points in both roles
  at the same level picks one role for that event. A dancer allowed to
  dance up in the primary role and choosing to do so dances one level
  below that in the secondary role [R2023a §3.2 #3, #7a-c; NL2022-09
  p.1].
- The advancement chart applies per role: "the points required for
  moving up (or down) is based on the points in either the dancer's
  primary or secondary role, not their combined points" [R2023a Chart 3
  note †].
- Newcomer gains a rule: allowed to move up at will; required to move up
  with 10 or more Newcomer points or 1 Novice point [R2023a Chart 3
  Newcomer row].
- Tier 3 finalist points stop at 10th place [R2023a Chart 4] (was 12th
  since 2018 [P2018 p.1; P2020 p.1; P2021web]).
- Competitors who fail to follow the Points Registry Rules forfeit
  placement points; unapproved dancing up or down is explicitly covered
  [R2023a §3.2 #1, #5a].
- Trial Events award no points [R2023a §1.3.1 #6; NL2022-09 p.1]; the same
  pre-2022 Trial point treatment is **unverified** in this account.
- Results due within 3 days, announced effective immediately on
  2023-05-16 [NL2023-05 p.1 "New rule change in reporting of scores"].

### 2023-07-05: windows removed, "one point moves you up"

Registry Event Rules 2023.1C, dated 2023-06-08 [R2023b p.1]. The
September-updated file named 2023.1D still prints 2023.1C internally;
its September 25 note identifies footnote-symbol changes [R2023c p.1].

- The 36-month windows are gone. Advanced: allowed to move up at 60
  Advanced points, required at 90, no time limit. All Star: allowed to
  move up at 150 All Star points, no time limit; the 3-points-to-stay and
  10-points-to-return rules are removed [R2023b Chart 3, compared with
  R2023a Chart 3].
- A single point in the next division now forces the move: Novice
  required at 30 Novice points or 1 Intermediate point; Intermediate at 45
  or 1 Advanced point; Advanced at 90 or 1 All Star point; Newcomer at 1
  Newcomer point or 1 Novice point (was 10 Newcomer points) [R2023b
  Chart 3].
- Advanced dancers who qualified for All Star under the freeze as of
  2023-07-04 could dance All Star through 2023-12-31 [R2023b Chart 3 note
  ‡; NL2023-09 p.2 "grace period"].
- The five-leaders-and-five-followers minimum now applies to the final,
  not the division [R2023b §3.1 #3, §3.2 #8a, §3.3.1; R2023a §3.2 #8a
  for the earlier wording].
- Secondary role: a dancer with little success in that role may petition
  to dance one additional level down. Event-specific; the secondary role
  is not a separate advancement track [R2023b §3.2 #7d; NL2023-09 p.1-2
  "Secondary Roles - petition process clarification"].
- The registry database shows primary and secondary role points
  separately from September 2023 [NL2023-09 p.2 "WSDC Points Registry
  database update"].
