# WSDC rule changes, 2024-2026

This chronology records written competition rules and their evidence gaps.
Start with the [rule-history summary](../wsdc-rules-history.md) for the eras
and their effect on the dataset. Source labels refer to its [source ledger](../wsdc-rules-history.md#sources).

### 2024-01-01: housekeeping

Registry Event Rules 2024.1A, dated 2023-12-17 [R2024a p.1, p.14;
NL2024-01 p.2 "Rules 2024"]. Mostly event rules.

- Petitions may not be used to reach a tier's minimum count, and dancers
  admitted by petition do not count toward Tier 1's minimum [R2024a §3.1
  #6b-c; NL2024-01 p.2 "Petitions"].
- Events may restrict how many Jack & Jills a dancer enters [R2024a §3.1
  #2b; NL2024-01 p.2 "Restrictions on contest entries"].
- The open-to-all-skill-levels condition for age-based contests continues
  [R2023a §3.1 #2b; R2024a §3.1 #3a;
  NL2024-01 p.3 recap].

### 2024-03-27: raw-score wording

The 2024.1B revision is effective March 27 and specifies numerical raw
scores. This does not change registry point awards [R2024a, 1B §3.4
note 6].

### 2024-07-01: Champion defined by points, petitions up centralized

Registry Event Rules 2024.2A and 2024.2B state effective 2024-07-01
[R2024b p.1; R2024c p.1]. The later version list records revisions dated
2024-03-20, 2024-05-30, and 2024-07-11 [R2025 p.17]. The July 11 update
clarifies driving distance and requires unique numerical raw scores from
the Raw Score Judge; it is not just a footnote change [R2024c Definitions
and note 1].
Announced as an interim rule through 2025-06-30 [R2024b §3.2 note 3;
NL2024-05 p.3 "New WSDC Champions division rules"]; still in force in the
2026 rules [R2026b §3.2 #11].

- Champion: allowed with 1 Champion point or 150 All Star points;
  required with 10 Champion points or 225 All Star points. Events may no
  longer set their own qualifications for a WSDC Champion Jack & Jill
  [R2024b §3.2 #10, Chart 3; NL2024-05 p.3]. Events that had published
  their own definition before 2024-05-20 could ask to keep it through
  2024-12-31 [R2024b §3.2 note 3; NL2024-05 p.3]. A dancer required to
  dance Champion may not enter All Star without a petition [R2024b §3.2
  #10b].
- Petitions to dance up go to the WSDC Chief Judges Committee at least 15
  days before the event with two videos. Approval lasts six months at any
  event. A dancer who then earns a point in the higher division stays
  there. Petitions down stay event-specific [R2024b §3.1 #6d-e; NL2024-05
  p.2 "New Petition Process for dancing up"].
- Brief role switching (under eight counts) for creative purposes is
  allowed [R2024b §3.2 #3a].
- A combined Newcomer/Novice contest awards Novice points; a combined All
  Star/Champion contest awards All Star points [R2024b §3.1 #7, §3.2
  #10b; NL2024-05 p.3]. Not in 2024.1A [R2024a §3.1 #7].
- A dancer may not dance down when their own division is not offered
  [R2024b §3.1 #7a].

### January 2025: Tier 4 cap, secondary-role choice, Trial Event points

Registry Event Rules 2025.1A, dated 2024-12-12 [R2025 p.1, p.17;
NL2025-02 p.2 "Rules 2025"].

- Tier 4 finalist points stop at 12th place [R2025 Chart 4; NL2024-10 p.3
  "Tier 4 shall only provide finalist points through 12th place"] (was
  15th since 2018 [P2018 p.1; R2024b Chart 4]).
- Trial Events award points, provided the Chief Judge is WSDC certified.
  Points stand even if the event never becomes a Registry Event. The
  new Trial rules apply to Trial Events approved after 2024-10-16;
  reports, surcharge payment, and compliance are also required [R2025
  §1.3 #3-4, note 2; NL2024-10 p.1]. **Unresolved date discrepancy:**
  the newsletter says points begin 2025-01-02; the final rulebook is
  effective 2025-01-01. Do not turn either into a claim about the first
  actual registry row. The recovered 2023 and 2024 rules expressly
  award no Trial points [R2023a §1.3.1 #6; R2024b §1.3 #3].
- Secondary role: a dancer may dance one or two levels down from the
  level they enter in the primary role, at their own choice, unless their
  secondary-role points require them to move up. Petitions for the
  secondary role are no longer accepted [R2025 §3.1 #6a, §3.2 #7b;
  NL2024-10 p.3 "Dancing down in your secondary role"]. A dancer
  competing in Intermediate or higher in the primary role may not dance
  Newcomer in the secondary role [R2025 §3.2 #7b.i, #7d; NL2024-10 p.3].
- The rule that a dancer keeps one skill level across all contests at an
  event is dropped; it only affected non-WSDC contests [R2025 §3.2 note
  4].
- Tier 1 finals must have five in the final [R2025 Chart 5]. Events may
  skip a round when a role is at most 10 percent over the previous tier's
  maximum [R2025 Chart 5 note 9].
- Surcharge announced to rise from $1 to $2 on 2026-01-01 (no effect on
  points) [R2025 §2.2 note 3; NL2024-10 p.2].

### 2026-01-01: secondary-role chart

Registry Event Rules 2026.1A (2025-12-30) [R2026a], 2026.1B (2026-01-17)
[R2026b], 2026.1C [R2026c], 2026.1D (2026-05-22) [R2026d]. These
dates are from the filenames/publication context; the PDF headers identify
the versions. The WSDC rules index gives 2026-01-01 as the annual
effective date [W2026]. The PDFs retain a conflicting final footer saying
January 1, 2025.

No points-rule change was found across the recovered A–D versions. D
repairs the truncated Secondary Chief Judge definition and makes wording
and layout corrections [R2026a; R2026b; R2026c; R2026d]. The primary-role
advancement thresholds and tier award chart match 2025; the secondary-role
chart is new [R2026b Charts 3-5; R2025 Charts 3-4].

- A Secondary Role Advancement Chart sets the secondary level from the
  highest level the dancer is allowed in the primary role, regardless of
  which primary level they enter. Two levels down are allowed until the
  secondary role reaches its own "may move up" threshold, after which
  only one level down. Petitions down are not allowed for the secondary
  role; petitions up are [R2026b §3.2 #6a, #8a-d, Chart 4].
- With no points in either role, a dancer may enter Newcomer in either
  role. Otherwise secondary-role Newcomer requires competing in Novice
  in the primary role with fewer than 16 Novice points, and no
  secondary-role points [R2026b §3.2 #8e].
- Combined skill-level divisions may not be offered at registration. If a
  division misses Tier 1 at registration close, the event may merge it
  with the adjacent division or cancel it. The combined contest awards
  the lower division's points, except Newcomer/Novice awards Novice
  points [R2026b §3.1 #7a-b].
- Dancing in the wrong division without an approved petition is a
  disqualification [R2026b §3.2 #6b].
- Per-competitor surcharge is $2 [R2026b §2.2; NL2024-10 p.2].
